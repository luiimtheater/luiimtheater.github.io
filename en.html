Lui Im Theater

Contemporary Taiwanese Puppetry,
rooted in local stories.

Lui Im Theater was founded in 2020 to carry Taiwanese glove puppetry into contemporary life through Taiwanese-language creation, local stories and Taiwanese opera-inspired music.

About

Tradition as a living practice

The company brings together puppeteers, actors, traditional musicians and arts administrators. Its work combines glove-puppetry technique, Taiwanese dialogue, live traditional music and contemporary theatre-making, while continuing long-term educational outreach in schools and communities.
